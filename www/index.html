📍 Auto start/stop your work timer?
Invoice & PDF can use your location in the background — even when the app is closed — to automatically start and stop your work timer when you arrive at or leave a saved job site, so your billable hours are captured without tapping anything.

This is only for your own time tracking. Your location is never used for ads and never shared. You can skip this and start/stop the timer yourself — the app works fine either way.
